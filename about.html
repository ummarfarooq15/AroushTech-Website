OUR STORY

Consulting and training
that help people and businesses move forward.

Aroush Solutions (OPC) Private Limited is a Bengaluru-based consulting and training company. Its stated vision is to become a preferred provider of consulting and training that helps people and businesses around the world.

The company focuses on practical learning, technical development, tailored training, and addressing workplace challenges. Its programs combine instructor-led learning with hands-on practice.

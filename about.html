OUR STORY

A vision for accessible
technical education.

Aroush Solutions (OPC) Private Limited is a Bengaluru-based consulting and training company. AroushTech’s published vision is to become a preferred consulting and training provider that empowers people and businesses around the world.

Its published mission is to support people and help them realize their potential through training programs. Courses combine instructor-led learning with hands-on practice and technical development.
